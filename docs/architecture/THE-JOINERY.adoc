== The maths

The formal content of cleave is small and sharp, and lives in
`docs/standards/RANKED-OWNERSHIP-CLEAVE.adoc` (RC-1..13 + TS-1..7, v0.3)
and `docs/architecture/CLEAVE-ENGINE-DESIGN.adoc`:

. **One surface, dial-positioned.** An integration surface's character —
  FFI-direct, sandboxed, protocol-mediated — is a *position* on a
  well-founded staircase of connection-steps, walked by a zipper. Two
  assumptions ride in the types: *WF* (there is a bottom `⊥`; descents
  terminate — graceful teardown becomes a theorem, not a hope) and *OF*
  (each step's loss is graded and known — transmuting never silently drops
  capability).
. **Soft/hard = descent depth.** Soft grooving takes a sparse,
  order-respecting subset of steps and lets its occupancy lease *expire*
  (zero-residue wipe). Hard grooving descends near `⊥`, docked, its lease
  *refreshing* on heartbeat (RC-6). One surface, two readings — never two
  implementations.
. **Self-duality.** `connect` mints down the order; `disconnect` linearly
  consumes up the dual; `⊥` *is* the zero-residue state. Join and clean
  separation are the same typed structure read in opposite directions —
  the construction/consumption duality of linear types, and the reason
  `cleave` (the contranym) is the right name.
. **Ranked ownership kills cycles.** Every owning edge carries
  `rank(child) < rank(parent)` in a well-founded order, so an ownership
  cycle would be an infinite descending chain — unrepresentable (RC-1..3).
  Only `owns` edges enter the residue proof; `borrow`/`observe` edges may
  cycle freely (RC-4/5). Termination of tree teardown is Dershowitz–Manna:
  replace a node by the finite multiset of its strictly-lower children.
  Finite trees need only `ℕ` — RC-11 *forbids* ordinal overkill.
. **Two-party completion.** One side reaching `⊥` is a half-closed
  connection, which is residue. Clean-cleave is both peers at `⊥` with
  dual acknowledgement (RC-9); the lost-final-ack seam is the two-generals
  problem, resolved as clean-XOR-rupture (RC-13) with rupture delegated to
  survivor-owns-the-wipe (RC-10).
. **The novelty: posture = f(dial).** Gating, permissions and
  preemptability are a *function of dial position* — monotone, atomically
  re-derived on re-dial, witnessed in types, never on the wire
  (TS-1..7, normative since v0.3). Adjacent technology fixes posture per
  interface *kind*; cleave makes it a continuous, ordered property of one
  surface.

== The art

The naming is load-bearing (groove ADR 0009, registered in
`standards/CANONICAL-NAMES.adoc`): one metaphor family — joinery.
`cleave` is the surface and a contranym (to cleave apart: clean,
zero-residue teardown; to cleave together: deep bond) — the dial from
separation to union in a single word. `groove` is the channel cut into a
surface: passive until engaged, guiding without constraining; how deep a
piece seats is how integrated, trusted and gated the connection is.
`spline` is the thin strip seated into a groove on both boards to carry
the join — it presupposes the groove and is the material that passes
through it. *Consumers are not layers*: gossamer, burble, panll keep
their own names and metaphors; the substrate is named for function, never
for its loudest early consumer.

== The science

The discipline that keeps the theory honest:

* **Falsifiers, not vibes.** Every invariant (RC-1..13, TS-1..7) carries a
  machine-readable "violated iff" clause. A claim without a falsifier is
  not in the spec.
* **The truth-ledger.** `docs/PROOF-NEEDS.adoc` states what is proven,
  what is mirrored, what is open — with scope qualifiers ("closed for the
  kernel path *only*") that CI and reviewers can hold against the README.
  The structure-check workflow greps the honesty line.
* **Executable conformance.** Groove's spec is an executable checklist
  (CONF-L1/L2 + the L2 lease extension, one test per ID, CI-enforced);
  cleave's kernel is an acceptance suite (KERN-1..7, names = IDs); the
  soft/hard evidence run is a script whose failure is an exit code
  (`groove/examples/soft-hard-lease-demo.sh`), not a paragraph.
* **The bar.** Cleave is "demonstrated" only where a point is wired
  end-to-end: real capability, both soft AND hard, descent actually
  invoked, handle actually linear, proof guarding running code. As of
  2026-07-09 exactly one point — the kernel — meets it. Saying "one
  point, no further" is the method.

== The engineering

What runs, where, today:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| cleave kernel | `src/` — dep-free Rust, KERN-1..7 green; Idris2 mirrors typecheck in CI (`cleave-proofs.ipkg`).
| groove | Reference provider + CLI (Rust, `groove-protocol`): discovery, connect/heartbeat/disconnect/mesh, hash-chained attestations, **lease modes** (SPEC §4.6) and **signed manifests** (SPEC §2.1.5, ADR 0010; Ed25519 over canonical JSON, registry-pinned keys).
| burble (provider consumer-side) | Working P2P voice + AI bridge; supervised groove provider with canonical generated manifest, leases, attestation chain (estate branch); groove surface at registry port 6473.
| gossamer (consumer) | Zig FFI + green Idris2 ABI; groove client with session API (`GrooveHandle` newtype, real connect/heartbeat), ordered teardown descent replacing the flat wipe (G-2's named offender), HMAC-SHA256, TLS fail-closed (estate branch).
| spline | Deliberately seed-only: ADRs 0002–0005 pin "parallelise" (plane multiplexing), Bebop schema ownership (burble's `.bop` canonical, spline owns the alignment), the typed token/handle boundary, and the promotion bar. No `src/` until the bar is met.
|===

The transmutation story consumers get: a gossamer frame and a burble
collaborator pair at *any* dial position — discovered sidecar (soft) or
docked embedded voice (hard) — and the same assembly seats into PanLL, or
extracts back out to a standalone frame, as a dial movement rather than a
rebuild. PanLL-side adoption (discovery panel per groove SPEC §8.1,
AffineScript; dialect/port reconciliation of its frozen-legacy backend) is
Phase 2; modelling attach/extract as `Surface::adopt` + re-dial on one
kernel surface is Phase 3.

=== Why spline is now cleave's critical path

Cleave's remaining heavyweight obligations — `O-6` (joint completion, RC-9),
`O-7` (clean-XOR-rupture, RC-13 general), `TS-5` (multi-process atomic re-dial)
and `TS-7` (posture never on the wire, currently *vacuous*) — **all require real
bytes between two processes**. That wire is spline. Advancing spline therefore
advances cleave; polishing cleave alone cannot reach those obligations.
Spline's own promotion bar (ADR 0005) doubles as the trio's integration test,
and its criterion (d) is the venue where cleave's `G-3`/`TS-7` discipline gets
demonstrated end-to-end.

NOTE: As of 2026-08-04 **the wire exists** — criteria (a), (b) and (c) are
shipped, so `TS-7` is no longer vacuous *for want of a wire*; it is vacuous
only for want of the capture that criterion (d) supplies. That capture — a
gossamer↔burble session whose bytes are asserted to carry no posture, rank or
lifecycle witness — is now the single unblocking step for `O-6`, `O-7`,
`TS-5` and `TS-7` alike. It is the next piece of work on this axis.

=== The discipline this round established

Three defect *classes* were found, each invisible until measured, each now
guarded:

. **Fake gates.** A gate that cannot fail is worse than no gate: it converts
  "unverified" into "green". Found in groove's proofs workflow (triple-fake),
  burble's `test-launcher.sh` (printed ✗, exited 0), and — as a *tooling*
  property — `idris2 --check`, which exits 0 on module-not-found and on
  unsolved holes (measured on 0.7.0 *and* 0.8.0). Gate Idris2 with an `.ipkg`
  `--typecheck`, never a per-file `--check` loop.
. **Never-parsed workflows.** gossamer's `e2e.yml` had invalid YAML from a
  sweep (indented `actions: read` under scalar `permissions: read-all`), so
  every run completed with **zero jobs** — for about three weeks, while being
  the CI this repo's own ledger names as the `G-2`/`G-3` verifier. Tells: the
  workflow displays as its *path* rather than its name, and
  `runs/<id>/jobs` is empty.
. **Generated mirrors drifting from their sources.** burble's Bebop generator
  *crashed* (so the codecs could not be reproduced at all) and its committed
  output had been hand-enriched, making `@moduledoc "Auto-generated from…"`
  false. The same class re-appeared immediately in gossamer, where new Zig FFI
  exports landed without regenerating `ForeignGen.idr` — caught by the
  ABI↔FFI cleave gate, which is what that gate is for.

The rule that follows: **every gate must be negative-tested** — break it, watch
it go red, restore — and any gate that is deliberately not armed must say so in
its own output, not just in a commit message.

== The triage (what is ludicrous, what is fabulous, what is necessary)

*Necessary and done this round:* the kernel; drift reconciliation at source
(burble's dual manifests unified + generated; ports against the registry;
the two-authority rule in `standards/PORT-REGISTRY.md`); ledger updates in
lockstep; spline ADRs with spline kept thin.

*Fabulous and done this round:* soft AND hard groove with a real capability
against the reference provider (evidence script green); TS promotion to
normative v0.3 with kernel enforcement; signed manifests; the tree-shaped
teardown proofs.

*Ludicrous — named, loved, deferred:* Bebop live-wired into burble's
transport (spline's promotion milestone, ADR 0005); PanLL panel-clades
build-out (gated on the Axiom.jl owner decision); GRV6/IPv6T as spline's
media framing; ordinal ranks and the general DM accessibility proof
(RC-11 forbids the former for finite trees; the latter is the G-1
residual, echo-types audit deferred); Groove v2 fully layered on cleave
(the LAYERING annex is the *start of a discussion* now that the kernel
exists — not a commitment); posture-lattice unification (TS open
question 1); burble's E2EE/PTP-hardware headline numbers.

hazard: the canonical repo is the *only* source of truth, and the estate rule
is **never restore a canonical repo from one of its copies** (copies lag; the
in-tree canonical is ahead). Recorded here so a future cross-repo conformance
job (the joinery enforcement step) can flag them mechanically. Measured
2026-07-27; verify before acting — this is a watchlist, not a diff.

* `hyper-repos/hypatia/src/ui/gossamer` + `hyper-repos/hypatia/test/gossamer` — UI/test fixtures.
* `hyper-repos/developer-ecosystem/rescript-ecosystem/packages/bindings/gossamer` — ReScript bindings package.

consumes the `gossamer-rs` binding) — **not** a vendored gossamer copy.
